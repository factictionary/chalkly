// what's inside

Five workflows. One chalkboard.

Chalkly condenses the entire tuition workflow — from syllabus-aligned test papers to digital fee receipts — into one fast, offline-capable app with a familiar blackboard aesthetic.

01 — Generate

AI Test Paper & Quiz Generator

Board-aligned question papers in under 30 seconds, with zero typing. Pick a curriculum, choose your chapters, and Chalkly assembles the rest.

  • Multi-curriculum: CBSE · ICSE · SPM (KSSM) · PT3 · IGCSE · State Boards · IB
  • Difficulty and question count: Easy / Medium / Hard, 5–50 questions
  • Step-by-step answer keys auto-generated with every paper
  • 1-tap PDF export and WhatsApp share to student batches
02 — Track

Homework & Assignment Tracker

Keep students accountable and parents updated with zero paper registers. Every assignment lands in the right inbox and updates in real time.

  • 1-tap assignment to a single student or an entire batch
  • Live status: Pending · Due Today · Tomorrow · Overdue
  • Interactive student portal for self-checked completion
  • Parent transparency feed and WhatsApp reminders
03 — Manage

Smart Tutor & Tuition Management

Everything you need to run a coaching class smoothly — from the first roll call to the last fee receipt of the month.

  • Batch and timetable scheduling by class, subject, and slot
  • 1-tap daily attendance with monthly reports
  • Fee register: paid, pending, overdue — at a glance
  • Branded digital receipts over WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS
04 — Revise

700+ Formula & Concept Vault

Master Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry with authentic chalkboard study cards. KaTeX-typed, fully offline, and organised by class.

  • Complete syllabi from Class 6 through Class 12
  • KaTeX typography for ultra-crisp mathematical symbols
  • 5 study modes: Flashcards · Active Recall · Quick Review · Spaced Repetition · Shuffle
  • 100% offline access — revise anywhere, no internet needed
05 — Connect

Multilingual & Vernacular

Built for local and global adoption with 12 languages, including Bahasa Melayu, Bahasa Indonesia, Português, Deutsch, हिन्दी, मराठी, বাংলা, தமிழ், Français, Español, and العربية.

  • Entire interface localised, not just menus
  • Generated test papers and receipts follow selected language
  • Vernacular-first for South-East Asia and South Asia
