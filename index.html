How it works

Three simple steps to digitize your class

No complex setup, no training needed. Get started in under 2 minutes.

01

Sign in & Add Students

Login securely with your Google account. Add students with their name, grade, subject, and board.

02

Track Daily

Mark attendance, record test marks, and write feedback — all in just a few taps during or after class.

03

Share with Parents

Share a unique 6-digit code with parents. They can view progress instantly — no account required.

Unique to Chalkly

6-digit parent access codes — zero friction

Each student gets a unique 6-digit code. Parents simply open Chalkly, enter the code, and instantly see attendance, test scores, and your feedback. No app install. No email. No password. Just instant transparency that parents love.

  • No account creation for parents or students
  • View-only access — privacy preserved
